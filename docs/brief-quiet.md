# Brief — quiet shop

What this page is for: a person in Berja who already half-decided, and needs the price and a proof of the cut before they open Booksy.

One action: Reservar.

How it should feel: you are already in the room. Marble floor, oak slats, the chair in front of you. One sentence, then the prices, then the cuts. The walk through the shop is the only thing that moves. Everything else stands still so it can be read.

Three words: quiet, specific, physical.

References, used as a feeling, not a layout to copy:

- winstonstudio.dk — one photograph, one booking line, no motion library. We keep the tour because the walk is the photograph. We do not add a second loop on top of it.
- East Nashville Beard & Barber, Heritage Barbershop, Scotch Pine — the cuts and the room carry the identity. Type stays quiet. Booking is one action.

What a 10 is: the first frame is the real shop, the next thing you can read is the price, the cuts are sharp enough to judge, and nothing else competes.

What this branch changes, and what it cannot:

- The ticker, the review belt and the 6s rotator stop. They were a demo stacked on the tour.
- The salon still is the photograph of a real cut on the marble, not the empty chairs.
- The archivo encodes the existing files at retina size, and two unused sharp frames replace two softer ones.
- The archivo is a still grid. The pinned reel dimmed every cut except one. The two clips stay as posters.
